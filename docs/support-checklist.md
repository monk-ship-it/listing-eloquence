# Internal manual-support checklist (not public)

Owner: Domenico Monk (CopyByMonk), domenico@copybymonk.com. Quill sends **no** automated cancellation or refund emails; every reply is written and sent manually by the owner.

## Cancellation request
1. Confirm the request comes from the account's sign-in email.
2. Point the customer to Subscription → Manage billing (self-service cancel works even with a payment problem).
3. If they cannot, cancel at period end in Stripe on their behalf, only after confirming identity.
4. Reply manually with the paid-access end date shown in Stripe.

## Refund request (draft policy — owner-authorised "reasonable refund", not yet public)
1. Identify the charge in Stripe (date, amount, plan); confirm currency is GBP.
2. Check usage: listing packs generated in that billing period (History).
3. Decide case by case and record the reason, e.g.: accidental renewal with little/no use; duplicate charge; service fault.
4. Issue the refund in Stripe manually (full or partial); never refund outside Stripe.
5. Decide whether access ends now or at period end; tell the customer which.
6. Reply manually confirming amount and that banks can take several working days.
7. Log: date, customer, charge, amount, reason, decision.

Fixed refund windows, statutory-rights wording, retention periods, correspondence address. Publish a refund policy only after these are settled.

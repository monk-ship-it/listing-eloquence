## Recommended cancellation and refund policy — ready-to-use wording

### Cancellation and refunds

Quill is provided by Domenico Monk, a sole trader trading as CopyByMonk. Contact: domenico@copybymonk.com.

This policy applies to Quill subscriptions bought for business use. It is a voluntary commercial policy and does not limit any rights or remedies you have by law.

**Cancel anytime.** You can stop your subscription renewing from your subscription page or by emailing us from your account email address. There is no cancellation fee or notice period. Cancellation stops the next renewal if we receive it before that payment is taken. Your access continues until the end of the period already paid for, unless you receive a refund for that period. We will confirm cancellation by email. If the account controls are unavailable, email us; you do not have to wait for them to work.

**First-payment guarantee.** If Quill is not right for you after reasonable evaluation, email us within 14 calendar days of your first paid subscription payment and we will refund that payment in full. Normal evaluation includes trying different voices and features. There is no three-pack cutoff. This guarantee is available once per customer and does not cover fraudulent or deliberately repeated refund claims.

**Unwanted renewals.** Email us within seven calendar days of a renewal payment and we will refund it in full if no new listing pack has been successfully generated in that renewed billing period. Failed attempts and free retries do not count as new packs. We will stop further renewals as part of that request. If you have already used the renewed period, contact us and we will consider the circumstances fairly; ordinary cancellation otherwise takes effect at the end of that paid period.

**Billing mistakes.** We will correct duplicate or incorrect charges, including a payment taken after a cancellation should have taken effect. These corrections are separate from the first-payment and renewal offers above.

**Service problems.** If a failed generation has used your allowance, contact us so we can restore the credit or provide a retry at no extra charge. If a material service failure prevents you from using the paid service and we cannot put it right within a reasonable time, we will offer an appropriate full or proportionate refund for the affected service or period. We will consider the duration and practical impact of the problem. You do not have to accept account credit instead of a refund you are entitled to.

**How to request a refund.** Email domenico@copybymonk.com with your account email and the payment date or receipt reference. Do not send your full card details. Once a refund is agreed, we will submit it to the original payment method without undue delay and within 14 calendar days. Your payment provider may take additional time to display it. We do not charge a refund administration fee.

Cancelling a subscription does not itself delete your account or saved listings. Contact us separately for account or personal-data requests. Nothing in this policy excludes liability or a remedy that cannot lawfully be excluded.

## Why this is the balanced version

Use the wording above without a hard three-pack ceiling. Three successful packs could be exceeded by a prospective customer simply testing the product's different voices. A sharp cutoff would create avoidable disputes over failed attempts, marketing retries and whether a pack was useful. The once-per-customer safeguard and clear distinction between evaluation and deliberate repeated claims are sufficient for an initial low-price subscription offer. Do not advertise an unconditional “14-day money-back guarantee” while hiding a materially narrower evaluation rule in small print.

The seven-day renewal rule counts successful new core listing packs only. A failed attempt, a marketing retry for an existing pack, opening saved history or logging in should not defeat the offer. Manual support can honour the policy initially; do not imply automated refund eligibility exists until it has been implemented and checked.

The refund deadline is the time the request reaches the support mailbox, not when the owner happens to read it. Keep a record of cancellation time, relevant billing period, new successful core-pack count, payment reference, decision and refund reference. Do not reject a request only because the customer used the word “cancel” rather than “refund” where the intention is clear.

## Privacy: internal preparation and verification

The operator/contact line is ready: “Domenico Monk trading as CopyByMonk is responsible for the personal information we use to operate Quill. Contact domenico@copybymonk.com.” The rest must reflect verified processing, rather than a generic policy pasted onto the website.

- **Roles and data map:** distinguish account, billing, support and security information used for Quill's own purposes from seller/occupier/property information processed for an agency. The agency will often control the latter; confirm the actual arrangement and processing agreement. Map audio, transcripts, saved packs, private notes, browser drafts, support emails and logs.
- **Purposes and legal bases:** record each purpose and its lawful basis. Contract may support service delivery to an individual customer; legitimate interests may be needed for business representatives, security or support; legal obligation may apply to accounting records. Complete the relevant assessments rather than saying every use relies on consent. Keep optional marketing separate.
- **Actual recipients:** verify the exact hosting, database/authentication, AI, transcription, payment, email, analytics and error-reporting providers used in production, including their roles and which data each receives. Do not assume that a billing provider is a processor for every purpose.
- **International transfers and contracts:** check actual provider entities, processing regions, subprocessors, contracts and any transfer mechanism. Do not claim UK-only hosting, signed data-processing agreements or safeguards without evidence.
- **AI use:** verify model-provider training, logging and retention terms and any gateway settings before promising that content is never used for training or immediately deleted. Describe Quill's AI-assisted generation accurately without implying that it makes final legal/publication decisions for the agent.
- **Retention and deletion:** owner must choose and implement justified periods or criteria for accounts, saved packs, audio, browser drafts, billing records, logs, support and backups. Test deletion, including provider/backups where applicable. Do not publish an unimplemented “deleted within 30 days” or “we keep nothing” promise.
- **Rights and complaints:** explain the applicable access, correction, deletion, restriction, objection and portability rights, and consent withdrawal where relevant. Give the operator contact and the [ICO complaints route](https://ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint/). Distinguish requests Quill answers as controller from requests it assists the agency with as processor.
- **Cookies and tracking:** inspect what production actually sets or loads and implement the required controls before making cookie or “no tracking” claims. Keep essential authentication/storage separate from optional analytics or marketing.
- **Security and incident handling:** document actual protections, access controls and the breach process. “Private notes” is a product visibility rule, not a promise that no service provider can process them. Avoid absolute security guarantees.

ICO registration is owner-confirmed and is not a pending question. Do not invent its reference or imply it removes the need for the above work.

## Remaining launch limitation and primary guidance

An actual business address remains unresolved. No further address request is needed in this draft; keep Terms/Privacy internally reviewable and do not present missing mandatory operator information as complete. GOV.UK says sole-trader invoices using a business name must include **“an address where any legal documents can be delivered to you”**. It also requires business identity/contact/address information before distance sales. [Sole-trader invoice requirements](https://www.gov.uk/invoicing-and-taking-payment-from-customers/invoices-what-they-must-include), [distance-selling information](https://www.gov.uk/online-and-distance-selling-for-businesses).

The cancellation/refund promises above are commercial choices for a B2B service, not an assertion that all business subscriptions carry statutory consumer cooling-off rights. Business customers can still have implied rights, and any mandatory rights remain preserved. [Implied and statutory rights](https://www.gov.uk/unfair-terms-in-sales-contracts/implied-rights). CMA guidance supports clear renewal/cancellation terms and fair refund handling for consumers; those are useful fairness principles here, without mislabelling the customer relationship. [CMA fair-contract guidance](https://www.gov.uk/guidance/writing-a-fair-contract-for-customers).

The government published subscription guidance on 19 October 2026, but the linked PDF explicitly says it is draft, the regulations require parliamentary approval and the new regime is not yet in force. It also excludes B2B contracts. Do not present future consumer-subscription rules as current obligations for Quill's business customers or rely on an unverified commencement date. [Publication](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/subscription-contracts), [draft guidance](https://assets.publishing.service.gov.uk/media/6acf97da2de3184ccf8b814e/subscription-contracts-rules-guidance.pdf).

ICO guidance requires truthful information about identity, purposes, lawful bases, recipients, transfers, retention and rights; it notes the guidance is under review following the Data (Use and Access) Act. [Privacy information requirements](https://ico.org.uk/for-organisations/uk-gdpr-guidance-and-resources/individual-rights/the-right-to-be-informed/what-privacy-information-should-we-provide/). Processing arrangements need the appropriate written contract, and retention must be justified rather than invented. [Controller–processor contracts](https://ico.org.uk/for-organisations/uk-gdpr-guidance-and-resources/accountability-and-governance/contracts-and-liabilities-between-controllers-and-processors-multi/when-is-a-contract-needed-and-why-is-it-important/), [storage limitation](https://ico.org.uk/for-organisations/uk-gdpr-guidance-and-resources/data-protection-principles/a-guide-to-the-data-protection-principles/storage-limitation/).
